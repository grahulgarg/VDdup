Cosmetic Dentistry & Smile Makeovers

Enhance your confidence with seamless tooth-colored fillings, immediate gap closures, and premium dental veneers. Crafting the smile you truly deserve.

Your smile is one of your most defining features. Over time, teeth can become chipped, mildly decayed, discolored, or develop gaps that make you hesitant to smile freely in photos. At Vital Dental, recognized consistently as the top smile makeover clinic Dehradun has to offer, we look at dentistry as an art form.

Cosmetic restorations allow us to fix imperfections dynamically and beautifully in a single visit without invasive surgeries. Whether you are replacing old unsightly silver fillings with invisible tooth-colored ones, or want to fix a broken front tooth with veneers, Dr. Pragati Singh uses highly aesthetic materials that blend naturally with your enamel. The result should be a repair nobody can point to.

Our Premium Cosmetic Services

We provide a comprehensive range of conservative and aesthetic filling solutions to restore damaged teeth to their original glory:

Composite Restoration (1/2/3 Surface)

High-quality, tooth-colored fillings applied in layers and cured instantly with light. Perfect for sealing cavities invisibly.

Direct Composite Veneer

A fast and highly dramatic smile makeover technique where tooth-colored resin is sculpted directly on your front teeth to fix gaps and chips instantly.

Pit & Fissure Sealant

A proactive, invisible coating placed cleanly over the deep grooves of molars to stop bacteria accumulation and prevent cavities entirely.

Temporary / GIC Restoration

A fluorine-releasing standard filling used often in pediatric dentistry or as a transitional restorative foundation before placing crowns.

Fiber Post Bonding

An advanced micro-structural procedure to internally stabilize heavily broken down teeth after extreme trauma or decay.

Why Choose Dr. Pragati Singh for Smile Design?

Building a flawless smile takes profound aesthetic knowledge and strict attention to detail.

  • Exact Color Matching: We use an extensive shade guide system ensuring your fillings or veneers are a 100% exact match to your adjoining teeth.
  • Minimally Invasive: Unlike older techniques, composite bonding preserves the vast majority of your underlying natural healthy tooth structure.
  • Painless Methodology: Extreme care is taken during cavity preparation using the highest quality local anesthetics for absolute comfort.
  • Shade and shape matching: Dr. Pragati hand-sculpts restorations to mimic the exact grooves and polish of natural enamel.

Patient FAQs

We believe in answering your concerns about cosmetic procedures openly:

What are tooth colored fillings?

Tooth colored fillings (composite restorations) are a durable, aesthetic alternative to traditional metallic silver amalgams. They blend seamlessly with your natural tooth, becoming completely invisible to observers.

Can you fix the gaps between my front teeth?

Absolutely. Teeth gap filling (Diastema closure) is expertly handled using direct composite veneers. We bond custom-matched resin to your teeth to close the gap gracefully in just a single visit.

Are composite fillings sensitive?

It is perfectly normal to experience very mild sensitivity to extreme cold for a day or two after deep fillings, but this subsides very quickly as the tooth settles naturally.

How long do direct composite veneers last?

With proper oral hygiene, avoiding aggressively biting hard objects (like ice or pens), and regular dental cleaning, direct composite veneers usually last between 5 to 7 years before needing a minor polish.
