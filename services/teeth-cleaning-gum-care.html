Patient FAQs

We clear up the common misbeliefs about scaling and periodontal treatments:

Does teeth cleaning make teeth weak?

No, this is a dangerous myth. Scaling and polishing simply remove harmful hardened tartar and destructive bacteria. Removing this infected buildup actually strengthens the tooth's bone foundation.

Why are my gums bleeding?

Bleeding gums are the first sign of Gingivitis, caused by plaque buildup along the gumline. If ignored, it leads to severe bone loss and loose teeth. A professional cleaning efficiently reverses this.

Can a loose adult tooth be saved?

Often, yes. Through treatments like loose teeth splinting, flap surgery to deeply clean infected pockets, and rigorous gum antibiotics, we successfully stabilize mildly to moderately mobile teeth.

How frequently should I get scaling and polishing done?

For most patients, oral prophylaxis is recommended every 6 months to maintain optimum health. Patients with braces, implants, or prior gum disease may require it every 3-4 months.
