Periodontics: Complete Gum Care & Cleaning

Prevent severe bone loss, instantly cure bleeding gums, and get sparkling clean teeth with advanced periodontal therapies at Vital Dental.

Did you know that healthy, pink gums construct the foundational support of your entire bite? Ignoring mild symptoms like gum bleeding during brushing can often lead directly to periodontal disease, a major cause of adult tooth loss. At Vital Dental, considered the best dental clinic in Dehradun, our Periodontal Therapy focuses exclusively on rescuing the tissues that hold your teeth tightly in place.

Professional teeth cleaning in Dehradun at our clinic goes far beyond mere aesthetics; it actively eliminates the crystallized plaque and calculus that daily brushing cannot touch. Dr. Pragati Singh, recognized as the best dentist in Dehradun, uses ultrasonic scaling, ensuring deep-seated bacteria are washed away painlessly, helping to immediately halt gum recession and stop persistent bad breath.

Our Periodontal Services

From routine maintenance to intricate surgical scaling, we cover all layers of gum health:

Oral Prophylaxis (Upper & Lower)

A comprehensive scaling and polishing session that comfortably removes hard tartar and coffee/tobacco stains, leaving your teeth smooth and vibrant.

Flap Surgery (Quadrant/Full)

An advanced micro-procedure for severe periodontal disease where gums are gently lifted to profoundly clean infected roots and regenerate lost bone.

Curettage (2-3 teeth region)

Deep localized cleaning below the gum line to aggressively treat localized deep pathogenic pockets and stop persistent bleeding.

Splinting of loose teeth

An emergency stabilization procedure using ultra-thin fiber ribbons to connect dangerously mobile teeth to stronger neighbors, preventing tooth loss.

Gingivectomy (Quadrant)

Surgical removal and reshaping of severely overgrown, diseased, or aesthetically unpleasing gum tissue to improve health and your smile line.

Patient FAQs

We clear up the common misbeliefs about scaling and periodontal treatments:

Does teeth cleaning make teeth weak?

No, this is a dangerous myth. Scaling and polishing simply remove harmful hardened tartar and destructive bacteria. Removing this infected buildup actually strengthens the tooth's bone foundation.

Why are my gums bleeding?

Bleeding gums are the first sign of Gingivitis, caused by plaque buildup along the gumline. If ignored, it leads to severe bone loss and loose teeth. A professional cleaning efficiently reverses this.

Can a loose adult tooth be saved?

Often, yes. Through treatments like loose teeth splinting, flap surgery to deeply clean infected pockets, and rigorous gum antibiotics, we successfully stabilize mildly to moderately mobile teeth.

How frequently should I get scaling and polishing done?

For most patients, oral prophylaxis is recommended every 6 months to maintain optimum health. Patients with braces, implants, or prior gum disease may require it every 3-4 months.
