Our Pediatric Services

Kids teeth are uniquely different from adult teeth and require specialized attention. Here is a breakdown of our advanced pediatric clinical services:

Milk Tooth Extraction

Painless and gentle removal of baby teeth that are severely decayed or stubbornly refusing to make way for permanent teeth.

Endodontic Treatment (Milk Tooth)

Also known as a Pulpectomy or baby root canal. We safely clean deep cavities inside baby teeth to relieve pain and prevent early tooth loss.

Preformed Metal Crowns

Durable crowns placed over baby teeth to protect them after a root canal or if the tooth is too damaged for a simple filling.

Space Maintainers (One Quadrant)

Custom appliances designed to keep the space open when a baby tooth is lost prematurely, ensuring permanent teeth erupt naturally.

Myofunctional Appliances

Early-stage corrective appliances that help redirect jaw growth and properly align emerging teeth avoiding complex braces later.

Fluoride Therapy

A safe, painless varnish applied to the teeth to strengthen enamel, remineralize weak spots, and highly resist cavities.

Habit Breaking Appliances

Effective solutions to help children overcome thumb sucking or tongue thrusting, which can severely distort dental arch growth.

Patient FAQs

Common questions from parents regarding our pediatric dental care:

At what age should my child first visit the dentist?

It is recommended that a child visits the pediatric dentist by their first birthday or when the first tooth appears. Early visits help prevent cavities and normalize the dental environment.

How does Dr. Pragati handle anxious children?

Dr. Pragati takes a gentle, "tell-show-do" approach. We create a reassuring, fun environment and prioritize making the child comfortable before starting any painless procedures.

What are space maintainers and why are they needed?

If a milk tooth falls out too early due to decay, a space maintainer holds the gap open so the permanent tooth can erupt correctly without crowding, preventing the need for complex orthodontics later.

Why repair a baby tooth if it’s going to fall out anyway?

Baby teeth guide permanent teeth into sequence, help with speech development, and allow proper chewing for nutrition. Untreated cavities in baby teeth can cause intense pain, severe infection, and adult tooth misalignment.
