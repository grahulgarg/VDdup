Advanced surgical care in a profoundly safe and sterile environment. From impacted wisdom teeth to complex cyst removals, experience anxiety-free clinical extraction.

However, when a tooth is damaged thoroughly by enormous decay, fractured beyond repair, or causing crippling wisdom tooth pain, extraction becomes the safest path to restoring your overall health. Our Oral Surgical Treatments

Our minor operating facilities cater to a wide scope of complex surgical dental needs with precision:

Simple Extraction

Painless and smooth removal of anterior, premolar, or severely decayed molar teeth using specialized elevators that preserve the jawbone entirely.

Wisdom Tooth Extraction

Expert surgical removal of impacted or partially erupted upper and lower 3rd molars. We eliminate your unbearable jaw pain with supreme safety.

Dry Socket Treatment

Immediate and soothing relief for painful dry sockets using specialized medicinal dressings placed deep into the healing cavity.

Cyst Removal (Enucleation)

Precise surgical eradication of cysts or tumors developing in the jawbone to actively prevent further structural damage to adjacent healthy teeth.

Fracture Reduction & Bone Plating

Trauma management for facial maxilla/mandible fractures utilizing advanced titanium micro-plates to establish stable, definitive healing.

Apicectomy

A highly focused endodontic microsurgery to remove exclusively the infected tip of a tooth's root when standard root canals absolutely cannot heal the massive infection.

TMJ Relocation (Non-Surgical)

Swift, expert manual relocation maneuver designed to fix a painful dislocated jaw joint safely without any surgical cuts.

Frenectomy

A quick, minimal procedure to remove restrictive connective tissues (frenum) under the upper lip or tongue, instantly solving speech issues or tooth gaps.

Surgical Exposure of Tooth

Guided surgical elevation to gently uncover teeth trapped severely under the gums, aiding orthodontists directly in pulling them into alignment.

Interdental Wiring/Splinting

Emergency trauma care involving strong wire ligatures physically stabilizing multiple severely loosened teeth after accidental impact.

Patient FAQs

We provide absolute clarity to eliminate the common fears regarding surgical extractions:

Will my tooth extraction be painful?

Not at all. Utilizing premium local anesthetics, we ensure the entire area is profoundly numb before starting. You will feel pressure, but absolutely no sharp pain.

Why is my wisdom tooth causing so much pain?

Wisdom teeth often violently lack space to erupt properly, becoming deeply impacted against other teeth or severely trapped under thick gums. This leads to intense pressure, facial swelling, and acute bacterial infections.

What is a dry socket, and do you treat it?

A dry socket critically occurs when the shielding blood clot dissolves or dislodges prematurely days after an extraction, causing dull agonizing throbbing pain. Yes, we provide fast dry socket treatment with intense medicated dressings for rapid immediate relief.

How many days will it take me to recover fully?

For standard simple extractions, patients usually resume normal desk activities the very next day. Complex surgical wisdom tooth impacts may require a soft diet and moderate rest for approximately 2 to 4 days.
