Patient FAQs

We provide absolute clarity to eliminate the common fears regarding surgical extractions:

Will my tooth extraction be painful?

Not at all. Utilizing premium local anesthetics, we ensure the entire area is profoundly numb before starting. You will feel pressure, but absolutely no sharp pain.

Why is my wisdom tooth causing so much pain?

Wisdom teeth often violently lack space to erupt properly, becoming deeply impacted against other teeth or severely trapped under thick gums. This leads to intense pressure, facial swelling, and acute bacterial infections.

What is a dry socket, and do you treat it?

A dry socket critically occurs when the shielding blood clot dissolves or dislodges prematurely days after an extraction, causing dull agonizing throbbing pain. Yes, we provide fast dry socket treatment with intense medicated dressings for rapid immediate relief.

How many days will it take me to recover fully?

For standard simple extractions, patients usually resume normal desk activities the very next day. Complex surgical wisdom tooth impacts may require a soft diet and moderate rest for approximately 2 to 4 days.
