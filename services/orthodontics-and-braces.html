Whether you are seeking completely invisible braces near GMS Road for an upcoming wedding or high-strength metal braces for your teenager, we tailor our approach. How It Works: The Orthodontic Process

Orthodontic movement relies on applying continuous, gentle, and controlled force to bone structures. The process requires an expert hand and careful, staged adjustments.

1

Digital Diagnosis

We use intra-oral scanners, specialized cephalometric x-rays, and photographic analysis to precisely chart out the exact biology of your bite and facial profile.

2

Custom Pathway

We choose a specific system tailored to your clinical needs—deciding between non-extraction arch expansion or extraction techniques to create the required space.

3

Active Correction

Through the use of brackets, archwires, or sequenced clear aligner trays, continuous gentle forces safely guide your teeth into proper alignment.

4

Retention

Once perfection is achieved, we remove the active appliances and deliver a custom retention system to lock the teeth in their beautiful new home.

Our Clinical Options

Orthodontics is no longer a one-size-fits-all approach. We offer a diverse spectrum of highly sophisticated systems, ranging from traditional high-torque steel to completely transparent computerized trays:

Self-Ligating Braces

Self-ligating braces utilize a highly advanced "sliding-door" mechanism built directly into the bracket to hold the therapeutic wire, rather than using elastic O-rings. This significantly reduces friction, allows for lighter, biologically friendly forces, and often decreases both overall treatment time and the number of clinic visits required.

Non-Self-Ligating Braces

traditional non-self-ligating (conventional) twin brackets offer massive 3D control. We tie the wire into the bracket using small rubber or metal ligatures. These systems are incredibly versatile and allow us to use colored elastics if desired, making them a popular, highly effective, and economical choice for teens and interceptive orthodontics.

Clear Aligner Therapy

For adult professionals seeking completely invisible braces, clear aligner therapy (such as Invisalign) is a revolutionary choice. Using a sequence of smooth, virtually invisible, BPA-free plastic trays, your teeth are gently shifted. Aligners are removable, meaning there are absolutely no dietary restrictions, and brushing and flossing remain perfectly natural. We manage both simpler non-extraction cases and highly complex extraction alignments using modern clear aligner mechanics.

Clear Retainers

The bone surrounding moving teeth takes several months to fully mature and harden after active orthodontic treatment ends. We provide high-quality clear removable retainers. These invisible mouthpieces must be worn meticulously to prevent natural relapse forces from deteriorating your newly perfected smile.

Every individual’s skeletal structure requires an expert treatment plan. Whether you are dealing with severely rotated teeth, spacing issues, or cross-bites, we are here to provide cutting-edge care directly in Dehradun.

Patient FAQs

Patient education is a clinical priority. Below are some of the frequent questions related to orthodontic correction strategies:

Am I too old for braces or clear aligners?

Absolutely not! Orthodontic treatments are highly effective for adults. In fact, discrete options like clear aligners and ceramic braces have made adult orthodontics incredibly popular for correcting misaligned teeth at any age. As long as your bone and gums are healthy, we can move your teeth safely.

How long does Clear Aligner Therapy usually take?

The length of your treatment depends entirely on the complexity of your bite alignment. Minor aesthetic adjustments can take as little as 6 to 9 months, while comprehensive cases requiring significant space closure may take 12 to 18 months.

Will I need an extraction for my orthodontic treatment?

Not necessarily. We offer both extraction and non-extraction approaches. If severe crowding exists, space may need to be created by removing teeth. However, modern mechanics and self-ligating braces often allow us to rapidly expand the jaw arch to create room without the need for any extractions in moderate cases.

Do I need to wear a retainer after my braces come off?

Yes, retention is a critical, lifelong part of your treatment. Without a retainer, teeth will naturally attempt to shift back to their original misaligned positions due to periodontal ligament memory. We provide discrete clear retainers to safely protect your beautiful new smile.
