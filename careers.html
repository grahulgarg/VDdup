Careers at Vital Dental

Build your career where dentistry is done properly.

We are a small, endodontist-led practice on GMS Road, Dehradun. We hire rarely and carefully — and when we do, we invest in the people we bring in. If you care about doing clinical work well and treating patients like people, we would like to hear from you.

See open roles 2 roles open

Why work here

A practice built around doing it right.

i.

Learn from a specialist

Work alongside Dr. Pragati Singh, an endodontist. Complex root canals and re-treatments stay in-house, so there is real clinical depth to learn from every week.

ii.

A modern, digital clinic

Chairside digital X-ray, rotary endodontics, apex locators and Class B autoclave sterilisation. You work with the right tools, not around missing ones.

iii.

Every Saturday off

We work Sunday to Friday, 10–2 and 4–8, with a long break in the afternoon. Saturday is always yours, every single week.

iv.

Unhurried by design

We do not stack patients back to back or rush treatment. The same respect for time applies to how we treat the team.

Open roles

Two ways to join us.

Both roles are full-time and based at the clinic in Kanwali. Salary is competitive and discussed openly at interview.

Front desk & chairside

Dental Assistant & Receptionist

  • Full-time
  • GMS Road, Dehradun
  • Freshers welcome

The first face patients see and the steadiest pair of hands in the room. Half front desk, half chairside — for someone organised, warm and unflappable.

What you will do

  • Welcome patients and run the appointment book by phone and WhatsApp
  • Assist chairside: prepare trays, manage suction, pass instruments
  • Run sterilisation — autoclave cycles and instrument processing
  • Handle billing, records and follow-up calls

What we look for

  • Warm, clear communication in Hindi and English
  • Calm and organised when the day gets busy
  • Assisting experience is a plus — freshers are fully trained
  • Comfortable with a computer and a smartphone
Hours: Sun–Fri, 10–2 & 4–8 Apply for this role

Clinical

Dentist BDS / MDS

  • Full-time
  • GMS Road, Dehradun
  • BDS or MDS

Clinical work in an endodontist-led practice, with the time to do it well — and a specialist a few steps away when a case turns complicated.

What you will do

  • Examine, diagnose and plan treatment with patients
  • Restorations, scaling, extractions, root canals, crowns and bridges
  • Work alongside Dr. Pragati Singh on complex endodontic cases
  • Keep thorough records and explain treatment clearly

What we look for

  • BDS or MDS (any specialty), registered with the Dental Council
  • Sound clinical skills and a gentle chairside manner
  • Fresh graduates with a strong internship are welcome
  • Fluent in Hindi and English
Hours: Sun–Fri, 10–2 & 4–8 Apply for this role
