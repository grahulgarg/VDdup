Top Tips for Oral Health from Dehradun's Expert Dentists

Small habits, big difference.

Prevention is always better (and cheaper!) than cure. As the leading dental experts in Dehradun, we often see patients who could have avoided major procedures with just a few simple daily habits. Here are our top gold-standard tips for maintaining a healthy smile.

1. The 2x2 Rule

Brush two times a day for two minutes each time. Most people rush and only brush for 45 seconds! Use a stopwatch or listen to your favorite song to ensure you hit the 2-minute mark.

2. Floss Like a Boss

Brushing only cleans 60% of your tooth surface. The other 40% is stuck between your teeth! Flossing once a day removes plaque from these hidden areas where cavities often start.

3. Don't Ignore the Tongue

Bad breath (Halitosis) mostly originates from bacteria on the tongue. Use a tongue scraper or your brush to gently clean your tongue every morning.

4. Hydrate for Health

Saliva is your mouth's natural defense against acid and bacteria. Drinking plenty of water keeps your saliva flow healthy and neutralizes acids that cause enamel erosion.

5. Visit Us Biannually

A professional cleaning every 6 months removes hardened tartar that your brush can't handle. It also allows us to catch issues when they are tiny and painless.
