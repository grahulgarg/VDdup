Dental Implants in Dehradun: Cost, Procedure & Benefits

Restore your smile permanently with the gold standard in tooth replacement.

Missing a tooth can affect more than just your smile; it affects your chewing, your speech, and your jawbone health. Dental Implants are the modern solution to this age-old problem, and Vital Dental offers the most advanced implantology services in Dehradun.

What is a Dental Implant?

A dental implant is a titanium screw that acts as an artificial root for your missing tooth. It is surgically placed into the jawbone, where it fuses with the bone over time (a process called osseointegration), providing a sturdy base for a crown.

Why Choose Implants over Bridges/Dentures?

  • Durability: Implants can last a lifetime with proper care.
  • Bone Health: They prevent the bone loss that typically occurs after tooth extraction.
  • Natural Feel: They look, feel, and function exactly like your natural teeth.
  • No Damage to Adjacent Teeth: Unlike bridges, implants don't require grinding down healthy neighboring teeth.
