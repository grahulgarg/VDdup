Finding Us: A Dental Clinic on GMS Road, Kanwali

Where we are, how to reach us from around Dehradun, and what to expect when you arrive.

By Dr. Pragati Singh, B.D.S., M.D.S. — Endodontist  ·  30 August 2026  ·  Vital Dental Care & Implant Center, Dehradun

Vital Dental Care & Implant Center is at Haripuram Colony, 2 GMS Road, Engineers Enclave, Kanwali, Dehradun, Uttarakhand 248001. GMS Road is one of the main routes across the west of the city, so most of Dehradun reaches us without crossing town.

Getting here

  • From Ballupur Chowk — head along GMS Road towards Kanwali; we are a short drive on, in Engineers Enclave.
  • From Prem Nagar and Nanda Ki Chowki — straight in along the Chakrata Road side and across to GMS Road.
  • From Clock Tower and Rajpur Road — west through Ballupur, then onto GMS Road.
  • From Clement Town and ISBT — north via Saharanpur Road and Ballupur.
  • From Sahastradhara Road and IT Park — across through the city to Ballupur, then GMS Road.

The signboard is lit in the evenings, which helps if you are coming for the 16:00 to 20:00 session. Parking is on the road outside.

Hours

Sunday to Friday, 10:00–14:00 and 16:00–20:00. Closed Saturday. The Sunday opening exists for people who work a six-day week, and the evening session for those who cannot get away during the day.

What the clinic is

A single-site practice led by Dr. Pragati Singh, B.D.S., M.D.S., an endodontist — a specialist in the inside of the tooth, the nerve and the root canals. That shapes what we do: complex root canals, re-treatment of failed ones and teeth other clinics have suggested extracting are handled here rather than referred on. Alongside that we do the everyday work of a general practice: check-ups and cleanings, fillings, crowns and bridges, dentures, extractions, children's dentistry, braces and aligners, and implants.

What to expect on arrival

A consultation is an examination, X-rays if they are needed, and a written plan with costs in priority order. Treatment needing anaesthetic is normally booked for a separate visit, so you are not rushed into anything on the day you walk in. If you are in pain, that is dealt with first.

Booking

Call or WhatsApp 92868 98353. Appointments are preferred so proper time is set aside, but we keep slots each day for emergencies. If you are nervous about dentists, say so when you book — it changes how the appointment is planned, and you can come in just to talk first.

Frequently asked questions

Haripuram Colony, 2 GMS Road, Engineers Enclave, Kanwali, Dehradun, Uttarakhand 248001, on the main GMS Road route, a short drive from Ballupur Chowk.

What are the clinic timings?

Sunday to Friday, 10:00 to 14:00 and 16:00 to 20:00. Closed on Saturday.

Do I need an appointment?

It is better, because time is set aside for a proper examination. If you are in pain, call 92868 98353 and we will fit you in the same day where possible.

Is there parking?

Yes, on the road outside the clinic. The signboard is lit in the evenings for the 4 to 8 pm session.
