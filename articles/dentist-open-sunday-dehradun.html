Looking for a Dentist Open on Sunday in Dehradun?

Most clinics close on Sunday. We close on Saturday instead, which is the point.

By Dr. Pragati Singh, B.D.S., M.D.S. — Endodontist  ·  2 September 2026  ·  Vital Dental Care & Implant Center, Dehradun

If you work six days a week, the standard dental week is useless to you. Clinics open Monday to Saturday and close Sunday — the one day you could actually attend. It is the single commonest reason people postpone treatment until something hurts badly enough to force a day off.

Our week runs Sunday to Friday

Vital Dental Care & Implant Center is open Sunday through Friday, 10:00 to 14:00 and 16:00 to 20:00, and closed on Saturday. Sunday is a normal working day here, with the full range of treatment available: check-ups, cleanings, fillings, root canals, crowns, extractions and implant consultations. It is not a skeleton emergency service.

Who this suits

  • Anyone on a six-day working week, who would otherwise have to take leave.
  • Parents, because children's appointments are far easier outside school hours, and a Sunday check-up avoids missing lessons.
  • People who work in Dehradun but live outside it, and prefer to travel in on a day off.
  • Anyone whose tooth started hurting on Friday night and does not want to wait until Monday.

The evening session matters too

The 16:00 to 20:00 session runs every working day, so if Sunday does not suit, an 18:30 appointment on a weekday usually will. The long midday break is deliberate: it keeps the two sessions unhurried rather than stacking patients back to back.

If you are in pain today

Call and say so. We keep time each day for genuine emergencies — severe toothache, facial swelling, a knocked-out or broken tooth, or bleeding that will not stop. Describe the symptoms when you call, because swelling and fever are prioritised over a chipped edge.

If you have facial swelling that is spreading, difficulty swallowing or breathing, or fever with dental pain outside our hours, go to a hospital emergency department rather than waiting for us to open. That is a medical emergency, not a dental appointment.

Appointments are better than walking in, because time is set aside properly, but if you are in pain we will fit you in.

Frequently asked questions

Are you really open on Sunday?

Yes, Sunday is a normal working day: 10:00 to 14:00 and 16:00 to 20:00, with full treatment available. We are closed on Saturday instead.

Can I get an emergency dental appointment on Sunday in Dehradun?

Yes. We keep time each day, Sunday included, for severe pain, swelling, knocked-out or broken teeth. Call 92868 98353 and describe the symptoms so it can be prioritised.

Do you charge more for Sunday appointments?

No. Sunday is a normal clinic day at normal fees.

What are your evening hours?

The evening session runs 16:00 to 20:00, Sunday to Friday, which suits most people who work during the day.
