Smile Makeover & Cosmetic Dentist in Dehradun

Expert dental insights and guidance from Dr. Pragati Singh.

Your smile is your most powerful asset. It exudes confidence, warmth, and personality. But if you find yourself hiding your smile due to chipped, stained, or misaligned teeth, a Smile Makeover at Vital Dental Dehradun might be the life-changing solution you need.

What is a Smile Makeover?

A smile makeover isn't a single procedure; it's a customized treatment plan designed to improve the aesthetics of your smile. It combines art and science to create a look that fits your facial structure perfectly.

Our Cosmetic Treatments

  • Porcelain Veneers: Thin, custom-made shells that cover the front surface of teeth to correct color, shape, size, or length.
  • Teeth Whitening: Professional bleaching that can lighten discoloration by several shades in just one hour.
  • Invisible Aligners: Straighten crooked teeth without the "metal mouth" look of traditional braces.
  • Gummy Smile Correction: Laser reshaping of gum tissue to reveal more of your beautiful teeth.

Digital Smile Design (DSD)

At Vital Dental, we don't guess—we plan. We use Digital Smile Design technology to show you a digital simulation of your new smile before we even touch a tooth. You become a co-designer of your own smile!
